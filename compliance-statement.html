Eleven controls, all in place. The first four are the ones a partner, an agent or an insurer will ask about first.
  • 1. Who contracts with the guest is fixed before the trip is sold — operator, organiser or licensed carrier. It is recorded in the trip file.
  • 2. Insurance responds for everyone on board, and the cover names the activity actually being carried out.
  • 3. The licence matches the country we sail in. Thailand is run with a licensed Thai operator and a Thai-registered vessel.
  • 4. The vessel policy names commercial charter use and covers every sea area on the season calendar.
  • 5. The captain's file is complete and in date — Captain 200GT licence, STCW, medical, radio certificate.
  • 6. Terms and Privacy Policy are published carrying our legal name, NIF and address, the package status, the insurance requirement and the complaints route.
  • 7. The four guest forms are in use — information, health declaration, signed risk acknowledgement, media consent.
  • 8. Safety briefing and risk assessment are issued for every departure, refreshed with last season's near misses.
  • 9. Host and creator agreements are signed — free berth, minimum seats, fee kept in full, image rights and the advertising-disclosure duty.
  • 10. Signed originals are kept and filed per departure, with a renewal calendar for every certificate.
  • 11. Every country row and every policy is re-verified before the first departure of the season.

Four commitments, in writing

  • Advertising is labelled. A free berth given for posts is advertising: the host uses the platform's paid-partnership label and a written "#ad" a reader sees before engaging.
  • Every guest is insured. A stated minimum — medical, repatriation, cancellation, personal liability and water sports — written into the Terms as a condition of travel.
  • The skipper's authority is absolute, in writing, including the right to end a guest's trip without refund for unsafe behaviour.
  • The carrier is named. Where a licensed operator runs the boat, their name is on the contract, the invoice and the insurance.

Review rhythm

Every country row and every policy is re-verified before the first departure of the season, then annually: the Terms and Privacy Policy are updated and re-dated on the website, the safety briefing is refreshed with last season's near misses, and the previous season's trip files are archived.
