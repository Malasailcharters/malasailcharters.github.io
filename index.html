Why travel with Mala Sail Charters

01

Small Groups

From 4 to 8 people on board. Enough to create an incredible atmosphere and get to know each-other. Guests have double-occupancy cabins, designed to accommodate two guests. With option to have the entire cabin exclusively for yourself, a single-occupancy.

02

Everything organized.

Boat, itinerary, safety, logistics. Our trips emphasize on expressive open-minded crew. We take care of the details so you just enjoy the week feeling supported, nourished and restored with the best daily routines, with attention to every detail and healthy vegetarian food.

03

Authentic experiences

Taking inspiration from culture & spiritual nostalgia and nomad life-style by the ocean. Not just a distraction but meaning slower emotional tempo holidays while sharing stories under the sun with the company of acclaimed hosts such as well-known expressive facilitators or free diver instructors.
