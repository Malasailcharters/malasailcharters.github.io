Why travel with Mālā Sail Charters

01

Small Groups

From 4 to 8 people on board, depending on the yacht size. Enough to create an incredible atmosphere and get to know each-other. Guests have double-occupancy cabins, designed to accommodate two guests. With option to have the entire cabin exclusively for yourself, a single-occupancy.

02

Everything organized

Boat, itinerary, safety & logistics. Our trips emphasize on expressive open-minded crew. We take care of the details so you just enjoy the week feeling supported, nourished and restored with the best daily routines, with attention to every detail chosen to set up on board before you arrive — including a few healthy touches like specialty coffee for sunrise to start your day gently.

03

Authentic experiences

Taking inspiration from culture & spiritual nostalgia and nomad life-style by the ocean. Not just a distraction but meaning slower emotional tempo holidays while sharing stories under the sun with the company of acclaimed hosts such as well-known expressive facilitators or free diver instructors.

Sailing responsibly

Beyond the sailing, this is my personal commitment: small choices that keep the sea as we found it, and that protect the places we visit.

SDG 12

Natural & biodegradable amenities

Refillable soaps and shampoos on board, free from harsh chemicals and microplastics.

SDG 12 · 14

No single-use plastic

Reusable water bottles and containers. No single-use packaging on board.

SDG 14

Reef-safe sun protection

I encourage sunscreens free from oxybenzone and octinoxate, which harm marine life.

SDG 12

Waste sorting on board

We separate and manage waste while sailing, and recycle at the ports we visit.

SDG 12

Local & seasonal food

Wherever we can, we shop fresh from local markets along the route.

SDG 14

Biodegradable cleaning products

Low-impact, biodegradable products for cleaning on board. No harsh chemicals.

SDG 14

Biodiversity with iNaturalist

We record the species we spot along the way on iNaturalist, contributing to marine biodiversity science.

SDG 13 · 17

One boat, one tree

Every Mālā trip plants a tree, giving back on land what we take from the sea.

SDG labels refer to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
