Host your own Mālā week

You have a community that trusts you. We have the boat, the captain and the sea. Bring a small group from your audience and host the week your way — a retreat, a reset, a content trip, or simply a week at sea with your people.

  • Your cabin is free — the whole week aboard, for you and your partner, yacht and crew covered.

  • You pick the destination and the dates — we confirm the yacht and publish your week, with your name on it.
  • Deposits start at five — as soon as five of your guests are interested, each one is asked for their 50% deposit and the week is confirmed.
  • We handle everything else — boat, captain, route, safety and the content of the whole week.

The Product

A week aboard, fully crewed

Mālā Sail Charters is a small-group sailing venue. You bring the group; we put one crewed yacht and one professional captain around it and turn a route into a week that feels curated rather than booked — a floating boutique with organic amenities and no crowds.

01

Small groups

Five or six guests on board — small enough to feel like a private trip, big enough to feel like a party. Double-occupancy cabins, with the option of a single-occupancy cabin for those who want the space to themselves.

02

Everything organised

Boat, itinerary, safety and logistics handled for you. The route is planned, the anchorages chosen and the yacht ready before you step aboard — you only pack and arrive.

03

Authentic experiences

Slower days, real anchorages and long swims — the weeks are often hosted by creators, facilitators and retreat leaders who bring their own community aboard.

What's In The Price

Included, and not included

Every Mālā week is priced per person and covers the yacht, the crew and her running costs. Provisioning, meals and moorings away from the base sit outside the price — a chef can be arranged on board.

Included

  • The yacht for up to six nights — modern sailing catamaran, 4 cabins
  • Professional skipper and captain on board (Captain 200GT)
  • Fuel, the yacht's base berth and her standard running costs
  • Itinerary planning and route design
  • Safety equipment, tender, paddleboards and snorkelling gear
  • Bedding, bath towels and biodegradable onboard amenities

Not included

  • Provisioning and meals on board
  • Marinas and any mooring outside the yacht's base
  • Alcohol and drinks ashore
  • Flights and airport transfers
  • Travel and medical insurance
  • Excursions and personal expenses

Optional extras

  • A chef or cook on board, to prepare your meals through the week

Tell us when you book and we will have it arranged before you step aboard.
