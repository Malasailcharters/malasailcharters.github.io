The Product

A week aboard, fully hosted

Mālā Sail Charters is a small-group sailing venue. We take one crewed yacht, one professional captain and a handful of guests, and turn a route into a week that feels curated rather than booked — a floating boutique with organic amenities and no crowds.

01

Small groups

From 4 to 8 guests on board, depending on the yacht. Double-occupancy cabins, with the option of a single-occupancy cabin for those who want the space to themselves.

02

Everything organised

Boat, itinerary, safety and logistics handled for you. Breakfast to sunset, the week is set up before you step aboard — down to the specialty coffee that starts the day gently.

03

Authentic experiences

Slower days, real anchorages, local food and stories under the sun — occasionally alongside guest hosts and facilitators who bring their own craft on board.

What's In The Price

Included, and not included

Every Mālā week is priced per person, all-inclusive of the boat and everything that happens on it.

Included

  • The yacht for seven nights — modern sailing catamaran, 4 cabins
  • Professional skipper and captain (Captain 200GT) on board
  • Full board — breakfast, lunch and dinner prepared aboard
  • Drinking water, specialty coffee and tea
  • Itinerary planning, permits, mooring and marine-park fees
  • Safety equipment, tender, paddleboards and snorkelling gear
  • Bedding, bath towels and biodegradable onboard amenities

Not included

  • Flights and airport transfers
  • Alcohol and drinks ashore
  • Meals eaten off the boat
  • Travel and medical insurance
  • Excursions and personal expenses
